Treatments and questions to consider

Creative Smiles offers teeth whitening and veneers for teeth that are discolored, chipped, or cracked. Depending on your needs, the conversation may also involve crowns, dental bridges, dentures, or implants. A crown may be discussed for a tooth affected by a cavity that cannot be repaired. The dentist can explain what a treatment is intended to address and whether it fits your situation.

Before you meet with the dentist, make a brief list:

  • Identify the teeth or areas you would like to discuss.
  • Note any pain, sensitivity, damage, or changes you have noticed.
  • Think about the result you have in mind, while staying open to the dentist’s assessment.
  • Bring your dental insurance details and ask about PPO or HMO plan acceptance.

Avoid choosing a procedure based on a photo or a description you found elsewhere. Similar-looking concerns can have different dental causes, and an exam helps guide the conversation. Ask about the proposed treatment, any alternatives, and whether restorative care is needed along with cosmetic work. That way, you can make a decision with information specific to your teeth.
