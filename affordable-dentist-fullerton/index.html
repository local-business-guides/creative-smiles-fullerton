What to ask and bring to your visit

The right next step depends on your teeth and your goals. A dental exam can help determine whether a problem may need restorative treatment, such as a crown, bridge, denture, or implant. Other services include extractions and cosmetic options such as veneers for teeth that are discolored, chipped, or cracked.

Before your appointment, consider this short checklist:

  • Write down symptoms, when they began, and what makes them better or worse.
  • Bring your dental insurance information and ask about PPO or HMO plan acceptance.
  • List medicines or relevant health details you want the dentist to know.
  • Note the questions you have about treatment choices, appointments, and follow-up.

Avoid choosing care based only on an online description or assuming every treatment is appropriate for your situation.
