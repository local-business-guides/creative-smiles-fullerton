What to ask before choosing a practice

An initial conversation can help you understand how the office approaches your concern. Share when the issue started and whether it has changed. If you have had dental work on that tooth, mention it. The dentist can examine the area and explain what treatment may be appropriate.

A few useful questions to prepare:
- What should I bring for my first visit?
- Can you evaluate this concern and explain possible next steps?
- Which restorative or cosmetic options may be relevant?
- Do you accept my PPO or HMO dental insurance?

Avoid deciding that a tooth needs a specific procedure based only on an online description. Several treatments can address different problems, and an examination helps guide the discussion.
