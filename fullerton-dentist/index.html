What a visit may cover

The right next step depends on an examination and a discussion with the dental team. Treatment may include care for cavities, a root canal, or a crown when a tooth cannot be repaired with a filling alone. Crowns may be metal or porcelain. For missing teeth, options offered at Creative Smiles include dental implants, full or partial dentures, and bridges. Veneers may be considered for teeth that are chipped, cracked, or discolored. Extractions are also available when needed.

Avoid choosing a procedure based only on a photograph or a description online. Similar-looking concerns can call for different care. Tell the dentist what matters to you, including comfort, appearance, and the condition of the tooth, then ask what options are appropriate.
