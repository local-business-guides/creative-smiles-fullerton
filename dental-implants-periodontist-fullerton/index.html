Implants, alternatives, and preparation

Dental implants are used to replace missing teeth. Other restorative choices available at Creative Smiles include full or partial dentures and dental bridges. The dentist can explain how these options differ, what each may require, and which factors matter for your mouth and goals. If a tooth is damaged rather than missing, ask whether another treatment, such as a crown or root canal, is relevant instead.

Before an appointment, gather information that can help the discussion:

  • Make a note of when the tooth was lost or removed.
  • List any discomfort, changes in chewing, or concerns around the area.
  • Bring your medication list and relevant health history.
  • Have your PPO or HMO insurance details available, then ask what coverage applies.
  • Write down questions about treatment stages, alternatives, and follow-up care.

Do not assume an implant can be placed simply because it worked for someone you know. An examination is needed to discuss whether it fits your oral health and individual circumstances. Ask for an explanation of the proposed plan before deciding.
